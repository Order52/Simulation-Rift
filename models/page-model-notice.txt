Rift AI's page model (page-model.txt)
=====================================

The model is numbers learned from the public data below
(docs/reports/page-model.md says how). It contains no page of the data.

Phishing Websites Dataset
  Ariyadasa, Subhash; Fernando, Shantha; Fernando, Subha (2021),
  "Phishing Websites Dataset", Mendeley Data, V1, doi:10.17632/n96ncsr5g4.1
  https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/n96ncsr5g4/1
  Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0):
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  80,000 saved pages (50,000 legitimate, 30,000 phishing from PhishTank,
  OpenPhish and PhishRepo, collected December 2020 to October 2021). Rift
  counts features in them (tools/rift-train/src/bin/page.rs) and keeps only
  the learned weights; nothing was changed in the data itself.
