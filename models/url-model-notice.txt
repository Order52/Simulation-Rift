Rift AI's URL model (url-model.bin)
===================================

The model is numbers learned from the public data below (docs/reports/rift-ai.md
says how). It contains none of the training URLs, except as noted for the
Public Suffix List and the most visited sites.

Public Suffix List
  https://publicsuffix.org/list/public_suffix_list.dat (fetched 21 Oct 2026)
  This Source Code Form is subject to the terms of the Mozilla Public License,
  v. 2.0. If a copy of the MPL was not distributed with this file, You can
  obtain one at https://mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/.
  The model carries the list's rules, with international names in their ASCII
  ("xn--") form (tools/rift-train/prepare.py makes them). The source form is
  at the address above.

PhiUSIIL Phishing URL Dataset
  Prasad A, Chandra S. PhiUSIIL Phishing URL (Website) [dataset]. 2024.
  UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cose.2023.103545
  Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Used to train the model; the
  model does not contain the dataset.

URLhaus (malware hosts), through the malware-filter project's urlhaus-filter
  https://urlhaus.abuse.ch/ and https://gitlab.com/malware-filter/urlhaus-filter

Tranco list of the most visited sites
  V. Le Pochat, T. Van Goethem, S. Tajalizadehkhoob, M. Korczyński and
  W. Joosen, "Tranco: A Research-Oriented Top Sites Ranking Hardened Against
  Manipulation", NDSS 2019. https://tranco-list.eu/
  The model carries 64-bit hashes of the 10,000 most visited sites, which it
  does not score.

Common Crawl (CC-MAIN-2026-39 index): ordinary pages, used as examples of
  benign addresses. https://commoncrawl.org/terms-of-use
